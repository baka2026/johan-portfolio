I like taking things apart to see how they work, then building something new from what I learn.

Technology hooked me through curiosity rather than a curriculum. I learn by experimenting: writing a script, breaking it, working out why, and trying again. JavaScript and Python are where I do most of that, from Tampermonkey userscripts that reshape websites to small tools that talk to APIs.

Cybersecurity is the interest I keep coming back to. Understanding how systems fail makes me a better builder, and I’m learning it the hands-on way on Linux, ethically and on systems I’m allowed to test.

I’m also fascinated by AI, both as a tool to build with and as something worth understanding properly. This site’s assistant, J.A.R.V.I.S., is one of those experiments.
